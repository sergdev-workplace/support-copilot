# How to Withdraw
Source: https://valetax.com/how-to-withdraw/

## [withdraw#steps] How to Withdraw: overview and steps
We’ve made fund withdrawals easy and instant, providing you with the ultimate convenience and assurance you deserve Login Now
Step 1: Log in to your Member Area
Step 2: Select your preferred payment channel
Step 3: Enter your account details and amount to deposit
Step 4: Start Trading!

## [withdraw#what-is-the-minimum-deposit-amount] What is the minimum deposit amount?
The minimum deposit starts from 8 USD, depending on the account type.

## [withdraw#how-can-i-deposit-funds-into-my-account] How can I deposit funds into my account?
You may log in to your member area and select the preferred payment option and deposit size.

## [withdraw#what-currencies-can-i-use-to-deposit-funds] What currencies can I use to deposit funds?
You may deposit using any preferred currency into our USD account, which will follow that day’s exchange rates.

## [withdraw#how-long-does-it-take-for-deposited-funds-to-be-] How long does it take for deposited funds to be credited to my balance?
Deposits are instant! You will be able to view your updated account balance within 60 minutes.

## [withdraw#when-will-my-funds-be-available-for-trading] When will my funds be available for trading?
You can begin trading immediately once your balance has been updated.

## [withdraw#where-can-i-view-my-deposit-and-withdrawal-histo] Where can I view my deposit and withdrawal history?
You may log in to your member area to view all transactions related to deposits, withdrawals and transfers.

## [withdraw#what-is-my-withdrawal-request-status-pending] What is my withdrawal request status ‘pending’?
This means your request in in queue and in the midst of getting processed. Once approved, you will be notified.

## [withdraw#why-was-my-withdrawal-request-rejected] Why was my withdrawal request rejected?
It could be that your account has not been verified, a possible error to data entered or insifficient margin to process withdrawals. We will notify you via email on the rejection reason to help fix this issue.

## [withdraw#i-have-not-received-my-funds-after-my-withdrawal] I have not received my funds after my withdrawal request. What should I do?
Should you not receive your funds within 24 hours, please reach out to our friendly support team for assistance.
