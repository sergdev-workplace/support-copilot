# EXAMPLE SOP: withdrawal not received (illustrative, NOT Valetax policy)

This file only demonstrates how internal SOPs plug into the knowledge base in phase 2
(`python kb/build_kb.py --with-sops`). Its content is invented for the demo and is never
loaded by default. Internal chunks may shape the agent note and routing; they are never cited
to customers.

## When to escalate a missing withdrawal
If the customer reports an approved withdrawal not received after the published 24-hour window,
escalate to senior support with the withdrawal id, payment channel and approval timestamp.

## What the agent must not say
Do not promise a delivery date or compensation. Do not ask the customer to resend card or bank
details by email.
